About Us

Holistic Paediatric Rehabilitation Centre provides child-focused rehabilitation and developmental support, helping children build skills, confidence and independence in a supportive environment.

Our Services

Occupational Therapy

Support for daily living, sensory processing and functional skills.

Speech & Language Therapy

Support for communication, speech and language development.

Behavioural Therapy

Individualised behavioural and developmental support.

Special Education

Educational support tailored to each child's learning needs.

Psychological Assessment & Counselling

Assessment and family-focused counselling support.

Physiotherapy

Support for movement, mobility and physical development.

Parent Training

Practical guidance to help parents support development at home.

Autism • ADHD • Global Developmental Delay (GDD) • Developmental Milestone Concerns • Cerebral Palsy (CP) • Neurological and Orthopaedic Conditions
